Who We Are

Not just designers. Not just developers. A full digital agency.

XENIX DIGITAL is a startup agency focused on technology, creativity, media production, branding and digital solutions. We work with businesses, organizations, churches, individuals and fellow startups who need their digital presence website, social feed, broadcast, or brand identity handled by people who understand both the craft and the platform.

Rather than specializing narrowly, we built XENIX DIGITAL around a simple idea: the businesses, ministries and creators we work with shouldn't need five different vendors to look and sound like one coherent brand.
